# Tagalog (tl): items for medical translator review

- **Register:** polite "po"/"kayo/ninyo" in sentences. Short buttons use plain "Oo"/"Hindi" instead of "Opo"/"Hindi po" to keep them short. Check whether "Opo" is preferred.
- **English loanwords kept:** staff, nurse, triage, triage desk, check-in, QR code, sign in/out, draft, care team, health number, synthetic data, clinician concept. Check that older readers will understand them.
- **"Clinician"** became "doktor o nurse" (doctor or nurse, which covers NPs). Check this is acceptable.
- **Seizure:** "seizure (kombulsyon o pangingisay)". Check which everyday word is clearest.
- **Shared option labels** (`new_findings`) must work for both the patient and a companion, so they have no "ko" (my). For example, "Pinakamatinding sakit ng ulo sa buong buhay" says "worst headache of a lifetime", not "of my life".
- **"Hindi ako sigurado"** (I'm not sure) means not knowing. It is not a "no".
- **"Between the shoulder blades"** became "Likod, sa pagitan ng mga balikat" (back, between the shoulders) for plain wording.
- **`takeover.who`** starts with {{who}}, which is filled with "Ang pasyente" / "Isang kasama ng pasyente". The capitalization is correct only at the start of the sentence.

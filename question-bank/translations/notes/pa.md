# Punjabi (pa, Gurmukhi) translation notes for reviewer

1. **Gender in first-person buttons**: we used slash forms ("ਮੈਂ ਜਾ ਰਿਹਾ/ਰਹੀ ਹਾਂ", "ਮੈਂ ਸਟਾਫ਼ ਨੂੰ ਦੱਸ ਰਿਹਾ/ਰਹੀ ਹਾਂ", "ਮੈਂ ਇਹ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਰ ਸਕਦਾ/ਸਕਦੀ", "ਨਹੀਂ, ਮੈਂ ਰੁਕ ਰਿਹਾ/ਰਹੀ ਹਾਂ"). Please check that these are legible on buttons.
2. **Second and third person**: we preferred forms with no gender, such as the ergative "ਤੁਸੀਂ … ਉਡੀਕ ਕੀਤੀ ਹੈ", "ਤੁਹਾਡੀ ਤਬੀਅਤ/ਉਨ੍ਹਾਂ ਦੀ ਹਾਲਤ", and the subjunctive "ਕਲਪਨਾ ਕਰ ਸਕੋ". Elsewhere we used the honorific plural ("ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਜਾ ਰਹੇ ਹੋ?", "ਤੁਸੀਂ … ਮਦਦ ਕਰ ਰਹੇ ਹੋ", "ਮਰੀਜ਼ ਜਾ ਰਹੇ ਹਨ", "ਨਰਸ … ਆਉਣਗੇ"). That plural is grammatically masculine; confirm that it reads as respectful and neutral.
3. **Clinician** became "ਡਾਕਟਰ ਜਾਂ ਨਰਸ" (doctor or nurse) because Punjabi has no everyday word for it.
4. **Seizure (fit)**: "ਦੌਰਾ (ਮਿਰਗੀ ਵਰਗਾ ਦੌਰਾ, seizure)". Check that it's understood without implying a diagnosis of epilepsy.
5. **Confusion**: "ਉਲਝਣ (ਕਨਫ਼ਿਊਜ਼ਨ)". ਉਲਝਣ can also mean worry or bother, so the loanword was added. Check this against what Punjabi speakers say to nurses (e.g. "ਦਿਮਾਗ਼ ਠੀਕ ਕੰਮ ਨਹੀਂ ਕਰ ਰਿਹਾ"). "Harder to stay awake" became "ਜਾਗਦੇ ਰਹਿਣਾ ਔਖਾ", and the companion form is "ਜਗਾਉਣਾ ਜਾਂ ਜਗਾ ਕੇ ਰੱਖਣਾ".
6. **Facial droop**: "ਚਿਹਰਾ ਇੱਕ ਪਾਸਿਓਂ ਲਟਕਿਆ ਹੋਇਆ ਲੱਗਦਾ ਹੈ". An alternative is ਢਿਲਕਿਆ. Choose whichever is more natural.
7. **Body locations**: "ਲੱਕ (ਪਿੱਠ ਦਾ ਹੇਠਲਾ ਹਿੱਸਾ)" for lower back (ਲੱਕ can also mean the waist), "ਢਿੱਡ" for belly, and "ਧੌਣ" for neck. ਗਰਦਨ is the more formal alternative.
8. **Last known well**: "moving" became "ਹਿੱਲਣਾ-ਜੁਲਣਾ" (moving the body and limbs), not walking. **"Last check"** in headache_change is the generic "ਪਿਛਲੀ ਵਾਰ".
9. **"I'm not sure"** is "ਮੈਨੂੰ ਪੱਕਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਪਤਾ" ("I don't know for sure"). It must read as unknown, not as a no.
10. **Triage** is written "ਟ੍ਰਾਇਆਜ (triage)". Transliterations vary (ਟਰਾਈਏਜ, for example), so check what local signage uses. **Health number** became "ਹੈਲਥ ਕਾਰਡ ਨੰਬਰ".
- **Triage card (`triageInfo`):** Level names: "ਬਹੁਤ ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ / ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ / ਘੱਟ ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ / ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ ਨਹੀਂ". The nurse sentences use the passive ("ਨਰਸ ਵੱਲੋਂ") to avoid a gendered verb. Check that "ਜ਼ਰੂਰੀ ਨਹੀਂ" (level 5) doesn't read as "you don't need care". No target times are shown on purpose; please keep it that way.

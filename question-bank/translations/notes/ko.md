# Korean (ko): points for a certified medical translator to check

- Register: formal 합니다체 throughout, with "~해 주세요" for requests. No 당신. The patient is addressed by leaving out the subject. Companion wording uses 환자, and the companion role is 보호자 or 환자와 함께 온 분.
- "Triage desk" is 중증도 분류 창구 and "triage nurse" is 중증도 분류 간호사 (KTAS terminology). Please confirm that patients understand these, and whether 트리아지 or 접수 창구 would read more clearly.
- "Check-in" (the repeated status check) is 상태 확인. "You are checked in" (enrolment) is 접수가 완료되었습니다. Please confirm the two concepts are not confused.
- Symptom wording to verify: drowsiness 깨어 있기 힘듦(자꾸 잠이 듦); confusion 정신이 더 혼란스러움 and 멍하거나 (for "muddled"); seizure 경련(발작); speech 말이 평소와 다르거나 알아듣기 어려움; one-sided weakness 몸 한쪽에 새로 힘이 빠지거나 감각이 없음; vision 앞이 잘 안 보이는 등 눈에 새로운 문제.
- "Clinician" is 의료진 (care team), not specifically a physician or NP. "Health number" (Alberta PHN) is 건강보험 번호. Please confirm both.
- "I'm not sure" is 잘 모르겠습니다, meaning the person does not know. "I'm telling staff" is 직원에게 직접 알리겠습니다 ("I will tell staff myself").
- "Lower back" is 허리(등 아래쪽). Korean 허리 can mean the waist, so the gloss is added.
- The companion version of headache_change is a little long. It could be shortened.
- exchange.takeover.who was restructured so the inserted {{who}} label needs no Korean particle.
